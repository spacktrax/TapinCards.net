What is Tap In?

Tap In is a review-card concept designed to connect your customer’s service experience with an easy invitation to visit your business’s review page.

How does a tap-to-review card work?

A compatible NFC-enabled phone reads a link from the card when held nearby. The customer opens the link and follows the review platform’s normal steps. Phone compatibility and sign-in requirements depend on the device and platform.

Does a tap automatically post a review?

No. Customers decide whether to leave a review and what to write. A tap opens the connection; their feedback remains their own.

Does Tap In guarantee 5-star reviews?

No. Great service earns great feedback. Tap In’s purpose is to make sharing an honest experience easier, without controlling a customer’s rating.
